# Nuclear strike

English · [Русский](ru/nuke.md)

A 1 or 15 kt nuclear warhead on an ICBM, a cruise missile or a B-2 bomb: flash, mushroom cloud, blast wave, fires, and
with a ground burst a crater and a fallout trail. The radii match a real explosion, with one block equal to one meter.

## How to launch

By default only operators can.

1. You need an ICBM item, or a missile or bomb plus a Nuclear Warhead ([recipes](munitions.md#recipes)).
2. On the console: the Nuclear weapon, or the "Warhead: ☢ nuclear" button of the Missile and the Bomb; yield 1 or
   15 kt; for the ICBM and the missile an air burst (wider destruction) or a ground burst (crater and fallout trail).
   The bomb explodes underground.
3. LMB in the binoculars or FIRE. A 3-second countdown follows, and LMB again cancels it.

The ICBM lifts off behind you and flies for 90 seconds.

If the world owner has allowed nuclear weapons for everyone, a player without operator rights needs a second key
whenever someone else is online: another player within 16 blocks presses `.` within 30 seconds. Every player is told
about such a launch, and it cannot be called off. The alarm at the target lasts at least 90 seconds: a missile or B-2
with a nuclear warhead takes off 90 seconds after the alarm starts.

## What happens

![Mushroom cloud over a town, the dust wall moving out below](img/nuke.jpg)

Everyone within 20 km of the target hears a siren in advance. Then come the flash, the fireball and the mushroom cloud.
The blast wave rolls out over the land as a wall of dust and smoke and breaks buildings as it reaches them: their pieces
fly with the dust. The ground shakes harder and harder as the wall comes closer. After a ground burst a short jolt
comes through the ground first, seconds before the wave.

Behind the wave a pall of dust climbs over the town for a minute or two, up to about 200 m high at 15 kt and lower
toward the edge. From a rooftop near the blast you first see the wave cross the town, then the dust swallows you. At
night the fireball, the glowing cloud and the fires light it orange, as they light the wall of dust. In the thick of it
you see only some 40 m and sounds are muffled. The dust settles over a few minutes and drifts downwind.
Where the light of the fireball set the town on fire, a firestorm builds up over a few minutes. Columns of smoke rise
over the burning blocks, lean downwind and merge higher up into a ceiling of smoke over the town. At night the fires
light it orange-red from below, and the glow flickers. A few times in the first ten minutes a fire whirl rises over the
hottest fires for a minute or so and wanders across them. From about the sixth minute the fires die down to
smouldering, and by morning they are out, together with the smoke. Ash and scraps of burnt paper fall around you.

![Fireball and blast wave over a town](img/nuke-wave.jpg)

At 15 kt the wave kills out to about 0.4 km, the light of the fireball burns out to 3.4 km, and windows break out to
6 km. At 1 kt all of this is several times closer. A ground burst leaves a fallout trail downwind, with black rain.
Fallout starts to come down about 7 minutes after a 15 kt burst, and later farther downwind.

![A town after a 15 kt strike: ruins and fires](img/nuke-ruins.jpg)

The land around the burst is scorched. Near the centre the wave strips the turf down to bare dirt. Farther out the
light burns grass, flowers and crops in patches and leaves black char where it was strongest, and the grass, leaves and
water around turn yellow-brown and grey. The edge is ragged, not a ring. The scorched colours stay for good, also in
Distant Horizons terrain.

The flash also marks what it lights. Wood, wool and hay that face the fireball char black in patches, and brick,
concrete, stone bricks and sandstone darken with soot. A player, a mob, an armor stand or a boat standing in the light
leaves a pale shadow of its shape on the wood or masonry behind it. These marks stay until the block is broken or
replaced. In the same instant lit lamps and substations of the [power grid](blackout.md) short out with sparks, and
grass, leaves and wood in the light start to smoke before the wave comes.

Over water the wave raises no dust. It runs across the water as a dark ring of ripples, with a wall of spray behind it
where the wind is strong, and white foam near the centre. A burst on the water or just above it throws up a hollow
column of water about a kilometre high at 15 kt. A ring of mist forms on the water for a few seconds, and when the
column falls, a white base surge rolls out for minutes.

Far away the blast comes as a sharp double crack and a deep boom, and then echoes back from the hills, forests and
towns around you: every slope that faces both the burst and you sends it back, later the farther it is. Animals around
the burst go quiet for a few minutes. Indoors the echoes are as muffled as the blast itself. Mobs run away from the
flash and the blast wave, then calm down; tamed, named and leashed mobs stay put, and mobs under cover don't run from
the wave. The town keeps burning for minutes: you hear the fires roar from where they burn, wind pulled into the fire
near them, ruins collapsing nearby, and now and then an explosion in the burning blocks.

## How to survive

- **Get underground.** The wave leaves natural ground alone: caves and bunkers stand, and earth stops both the light
  and the radiation.
- **Do not look at the fireball.** Its light kills and burns only those under open sky who can see it.
- **If you see a flash in the distance**, you have seconds before the wave arrives: get behind a slope, into a
  basement, into a pit.
- **Leave the fallout trail.** Water washes the dust off you (rain does not). Radiation sickness starts later, and milk
  does not cure it.

A Geiger Counter in hand clicks and shows your dose. Operators give it out.
