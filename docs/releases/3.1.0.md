For Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.259 or newer. Not network-compatible with 3.0.1: update the mod on the server and for every player.

## Changes

- The weapons are called by their real names. The designator menu reads Shahed, Lancet, Missile, Grad, Bomb, Nuclear, and launch and salvo messages, subtitles, tooltips and settings use the same names. Command words and item ids stay the same, so worlds, commands and command blocks keep working.
- The nuclear blast wave rolls out over the land as a wall of dust and smoke. Pieces of the buildings it breaks fly with the dust, and the ground shakes harder as the wall comes closer.
- Fallout starts to come down only when its largest particles reach the ground: about 7 minutes after a 15 kt burst, and later farther downwind.
- The ICBM climbs like a real one: slowly off the pad, faster as its fuel burns, straight out of the silo and then nearly vertical. It stays in the sky for about nine seconds instead of four. The time to impact is the same.
- A SAM interceptor turns toward its target in a short arc after it leaves the canister, instead of a sharp bend.
- Projectiles and interceptors fly smoothly on the client even when the server is slow, and face their course from the first frame.
- `/incoming <weapon> at <x z>` strikes a place on the ground: the server finds its height, as for a place picked on the map. `at <x y z>` strikes exactly that point; above the ground the round bursts in the air. Salvos and missions take both forms.
- A projectile that fails to enter the world no longer keeps its chunks loaded.

## Installation

Replace `incoming-3.0.1.jar` in the `mods` folder with `incoming-3.1.0.jar`. The server and every player need the same file; see the [README](https://github.com/zentixua/incoming#installation).
