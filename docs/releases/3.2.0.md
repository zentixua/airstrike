For Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.259 or newer. Not network-compatible with 3.1.0: update the mod on the server and for every player.

## Changes

- Behind the nuclear blast wave a pall of dust climbs over the town, up to about 200 m high at 15 kt, and fills the valleys around it. Inside it you see some 40 m, distant sounds are muffled, and ash and scraps of burnt paper fall around you. At night the fireball and the fires light it orange. The pall settles over a few minutes and drifts downwind. The new setting Nuclear dust pall (MEDIUM by default) sets its detail; OFF leaves only the dust wall and falling ash.
- Where the flash set the town on fire, a firestorm builds up over a few minutes. Columns of smoke lean downwind and merge into a ceiling of smoke, which the fires light orange-red from below at night; from a couple of kilometres away the town glows under its smoke. A few fire whirls rise over the hottest fires. From about the sixth minute the fires die down, and by morning they are out. A server with `nuclear.fires` off has no firestorm.
- The land around a nuclear burst is scorched. Near the centre the wave strips the turf to bare dirt. Farther out the light burns grass, flowers and crops in patches and leaves black char where it was strongest, and grass, leaves and water turn yellow-brown and grey in three new biomes: Singed, Scorched and Charred Land. The edge is ragged, not a ring, and the colors stay for good, also in Distant Horizons terrain.
- The flash marks what it lights. Wood, wool and hay facing the fireball char black in patches, and brick, concrete, stone bricks and sandstone darken with soot. A player, a mob, an armor stand or a boat in the light leaves a pale shadow of its shape on the wall behind it. The marks stay until the block is broken or replaced. In the same instant lit lamps and substations short out with sparks in the districts the strike blacks out, and grass, leaves and wood in the light start to smoke before the wave comes.
- Away from the fireball the blast wave moves at 0.42 of the speed of sound instead of about a sixth, so it reaches distant towns much sooner. The world setting `nuclear.wave_speed` changes this: 1.0 is true to life, and lower gives the server more time for the ruins.
- Far away the blast comes as a double crack and a deep boom, then echoes back from the slopes around you; indoors the echoes are muffled. A ground burst sends a short jolt through the ground seconds before the wave. Afterwards the burning town roars, wind rushes into the fires, ruins collapse nearby, explosions go off in the burning blocks now and then, and animals near the burst go quiet for a few minutes. Mobs run from the flash and the wave, then calm down; tamed, named and leashed mobs stay put, and mobs under cover don't run from the wave.
- Over water the blast wave raises no dust: it runs across the water as a dark ring of ripples, with a wall of spray behind it and foam near the centre. A burst on or just above the water throws up a hollow column of water about a kilometre high at 15 kt, then a ring of mist and a white base surge that rolls out for minutes.
- Ruins of the chunks a player sees come first and keep up with the blast wave, also on a busy server, where they used to appear two or three seconds after it. A chunk that changed while its ruins were being prepared no longer stays intact, and floor slabs no longer hang in the air next to a tower's standing core.
- Operators and the world host can pick a launch site on the targeting map: Ctrl+LMB, or **Launch site…** and a click. When they fire from the map or with the console target On map, a launcher sets up at that spot, turns toward the target (or the first route point) and stays until Abort. The route starts at the launch site, the B-2 comes in from that side, and the ICBM still takes off from its own site. The site stays for the next strikes until **Launch from me** removes it. It must be within 10,000 blocks of you, and a designator with linked launchers does not use it.
- The glow around the fireball and other large glows are smooth and no longer break into squares.
- Flashback replays recorded with 3.1.0 play with this version, nuclear strikes included.
- A bunker buster could stop underground without exploding and keep its chunks loaded until the server stopped. It now drills on and explodes.

## Installation

Replace `incoming-3.1.0.jar` in the `mods` folder with `incoming-3.2.0.jar`. The server and every player need the same file; see the [README](https://github.com/zentixua/incoming#installation).
